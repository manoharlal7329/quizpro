Registration & Login

How to Create Your Account

Getting started with QuizPro is quick and completely secure. No complex forms, no OTP hassle — just your mobile number and a password.

  • 📱 Open the Login Page — Enter your 10-digit Mobile Number that hasn't been registered before.
  • 🔒 Create a Password — Set a strong password to secure your account.
  • ✅ Login Instantly — If you already have an account, simply enter the same number and password.
  • 🎯 Access Your Dashboard — You'll immediately land on your personal dashboard with your wallet, sessions, and referral link.
  • 👤 Get Your Referral Link — Upon registration, you receive a unique referral link to share with friends and earn passive income.
💡 Tip: If someone referred you, make sure to register through their referral link so they receive their commission.
2

Wallet & Instant Deposit

How to Add Points

To participate in quizzes, you need points in your Deposit Wallet. 1 Point = ₹1. The deposit process is completely instant.

  • 💳 Open Wallet Section — Click on "Account" or "Wallet" from the menu.
  • 📷 Scan QR Code — Pay using PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, or any BHIM UPI app by scanning our QR code.
  • 🔢 Enter UTR Number — After payment, enter the 12-digit UTR/Transaction ID in the wallet.
  • ⚡ Instant Credit — As soon as you enter the UTR, points are automatically added to your Deposit Wallet.
⚠️ Important: There are 2 Wallets — Deposit Wallet (for quiz entry fees) and Winnings Wallet (for your prize money). Deposit Wallet is used to join quizzes, Winnings Wallet is used for withdrawals.

How to Play (The Arena)

Live Quiz Process

When a quiz starts, it's a real-time battle. Both speed and accuracy matter. Luck plays absolutely zero role here.

  • 📋 Join a Session — Browse upcoming sessions on your Dashboard. Press the Join button to pay the entry fee and reserve your spot.
  • ⏰ Quiz Starts on Time — The quiz begins automatically at the scheduled time. If you're late, you may miss questions.
  • ⏱️ 15-Second Timer — Every question has a strict 15-second time limit. If time runs out, it auto-submits with no answer.
  • 🎯 Speed Bonus — The faster you answer correctly, the more score points you earn. Accuracy + Speed both matter.
  • ❌ Wrong Answer = 0 Points — No negative marking, but you won't earn any score for incorrect answers.
  • 🏆 Real-Time Leaderboard — As soon as the quiz ends, final rankings are shown and prizes are distributed automatically.
